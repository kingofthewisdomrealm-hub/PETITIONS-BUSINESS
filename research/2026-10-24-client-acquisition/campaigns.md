Rules followed: every entry is limited to what an opened source states. "not found" = I did not find it in a source I opened. Contacts are only campaign/public business contacts that a source printed. Status key: **CIRC** = cleared and circulating; **CLEARED** = approved to circulate but activity unconfirmed; **PLANNED** = filed or announced, not yet cleared; **EXPIRED** = the circulation window is closed or the measure failed.

## Notes

**Legal and process points that matter for selling services**
- **Maine** petitions stay valid for 18 months from issuance, so the filing deadline for each 2027 measure differs: April 24, May 3 and June 8, 2027 (SOS page). Requirement: 67,682 = 10% of the 2022 governor vote.
- **Ohio** lets campaigns carry unsubmitted signatures into the next year. That is why all four Ohio efforts rolled to 2027. Amendments need signatures from 44 of 88 counties.
- **California** gives a 180-day circulation window from clearance. Two measures are live now with hard deadlines of Jan 11 and Mar 24, 2027. The 874,641 threshold implies a seven-figure paid drive at typical CA rates. No funding figures are published yet.
- **Massachusetts** is the most urgent paid-gathering market right now. 14 certified 2028 petitions need 74,574 signatures to local clerks by **Nov 18, 2026**. The local vendor ecosystem (SignatureDrive.com, JEF Associates) is entrenched, and several of these petitions were filed by the vendors themselves without backers.
- **Washington** IL27: the deadline is Dec 31, 2026 (the SOS page says this, not "10 days before session"). I found **no confirmed IL27 filer**. The VoteWA list would not render, so check it manually.
- **Florida** HB 1205: circulators must be registered Florida residents and US citizens, there is a $50k/violation fine on sponsors, and petitions must be returned within 10 days. Josias, as a FL resident, can legally circulate, but out-of-state crews cannot. A federal trial ended Feb 2026; the ruling outcome was not found.
- **Oregon** 2028: every filed petition is still pre-circulation (1,000 sponsorship signatures, then ballot title). The earliest is IP 2028-034 (draft title Oct 14, 2026). Thresholds of ~117k statute / ~156k constitution are 2026 figures.
- **Chicago**: nominating-petition circulation ends Oct 26, 2026. The remaining work is petition review and objections, which run until certification on Dec 17.

**Political balance in the list.** Right-leaning or conservative: SAM Action cannabis repeal (ME), Ax Ohio Tax, Mannix measures (OR), Underwood fuel and property tax (NE), Americans for Citizen Voting (MI), Shortsleeve income tax and MA tax cuts, Repeal 188 (MA), Let's Go WA, McKinney recall. Left-leaning or progressive: Healthcare for All Maine, Ohio Equal Rights, Florida Decides Healthcare, Missouri Right to Education, One Fair Wage, OR 2028-034 corporate-spending ban, MA collective-bargaining petitions. Cross-partisan or populist: Conserve Ohio data-center ban, qualified immunity (OH), CA top-two repeal, Rank MI Vote, OR open primaries, CA food-tax exemption.

**Gaps (not researched; the search budget ran out):** Arizona 2028 filings (the SOS page lists none yet), Nevada (an open-primaries/redistricting PAC filing was seen in a headline but not opened), Arkansas, Alaska (an Oct 14, 2026 ADN story on a candidate-signature initiative could not be fetched), Montana, South Dakota, North Dakota, Idaho, Colorado 2027 odd-year measures, Denver/LA/other spring 2027 municipal races, and most local recalls. Paid-circulation evidence for the Ohio Equal Rights, Undo the Top Two and Pimentel campaigns was not found. Check their campaign-finance filings (OH SOS, CA Cal-Access).
