# Paid Petition Circulation: State Compliance Flags (as of Oct 24, 2026)

**This is a list of compliance flags, not legal advice.** Laws and injunctions in this area change during a single cycle. Before any engagement, check the current statute, the SOS guidance and the case docket, and have election counsel licensed in that state review the plan.

**Scope:** statewide initiatives, plus Illinois/Chicago candidate petitions. "PPS" means pay-per-signature (any pay based on the number of signatures, sheets or valid signatures).

**Confidence key:** **V** = verified this session from an official or primary source or a reputable news report (URL given). **M** = from background knowledge, not re-verified this session. **?** = unresolved or status unknown.

**Research limit:** the session hit its web-search cap before every item could be checked. Items marked ? or M should be verified before you rely on them.

## 2. Key notes by state (with sources)

### Florida (home market, highest risk)
- HB 1205 (2025) requires circulators to be Florida residents and US citizens, bars felons whose rights haven't been restored, requires all circulators to register with the Division of Elections, allows a $50,000 fine per violation against organizations, cuts the turn-in deadline from 30 days to 10, and makes filling in a signer's missing information a felony. [Florida Phoenix, Feb 20 2026](https://floridaphoenix.com/2026/02/20/federal-ruling-in-florida-ballot-initiative-restriction-challenge-could-have-national-implications/); [Florida Phoenix, Apr 30 2026](https://floridaphoenix.com/2026/04/30/federal-judge-upholds-floridas-citizen-initiative-restrictions/)
- Paying circulators per petition form is a third-degree felony (s.104.186, created 2019, amended 2022). [flsenate.gov](https://flsenate.gov/Laws/statutes/2024/104.186)
- HB 1205 added mandatory training and a registration rule for anyone holding more than 25 sheets, plus an affidavit that the circulator was not paid per signature. [Ballotpedia: Petitioner](https://ballotpedia.org/Petitioner); [Florida Phoenix, July 2025](https://floridaphoenix.com/2025/07/09/federal-judge-issues-mixed-ruling-on-floridas-new-ballot-initiative-law/)
- Litigation: on July 8, 2025, Judge Walker partly blocked the non-resident and non-citizen bans. **On Sept 9, 2025, the 11th Circuit stayed that block 2-1, so the law is in effect.** Trial was Feb 9, 2026; the final order for Florida came Apr 30, 2026. Plaintiffs appealed June 1, 2026, and Florida filed its answer brief Oct 13, 2026. [Democracy Docket case page](https://www.democracydocket.com/cases/florida-direct-democracy-restrictions-challenge/); [WLRN](https://www.wlrn.org/law-justice/2025-09-11/florida-ballot-initiatives-noncitizens-nonresidents); [Ballot Access News](https://ballot-access.org/?p=93595)

### Arkansas
- June 30, 2026 summary judgment struck: the read-the-ballot-title requirement, the scripted fraud warning, voter-ID verification, **public disclosure of paid canvassers' info before gathering**, extra affidavits, the pause during the cure period, and the publication-cost reimbursement. [Talk Business](https://talkbusiness.net/2026/07/federal-judge-again-strikes-down-parts-of-arkansas-initiative-referendum-rules/)
- Aug 21, 2026 (after trial): **permanently enjoined** the canvasser residency rule (s.7-9-103), the paid-canvasser domicile rule (HB 1574, 2025) and the **PPS ban (s.7-9-601)**. The AG intends to appeal; the 8th Cir. hears the preliminary-injunction appeal in fall 2026. [News From The States](https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/federal-judge-permanently-blocks-arkansas-laws-restricting-direct-democracy-process); [Judgment PDF](https://www.democracydocket.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2026-08-21-Judgment-1.pdf); [UALR Public Radio, Nov 2025](https://www.ualrpublicradio.org/local-regional-news/2025-11-20/judge-strikes-down-canvassing-laws)
- **Flag:** an appeals court could stay these injunctions mid-cycle, as happened in Florida.

[Ballotpedia News](https://news.ballotpedia.org/2025/05/28/oklahoma-enacts-pay-per-signature-ban-residency-requirement-signature-distribution-requirement-and-funding-disclosure-rules-for-ballot-initiative-petitions/); [OK Policy](https://okpolicy.org/senate-bill-1027)

## 3. Open items to verify before selling into a state
1. **Oklahoma:** has the OK Supreme Court ruled on SB 1027, and is the registered-voter requirement being enforced?
2. **Massachusetts:** was S.2916 (PPS ban) enacted and signed, and when does it take effect?
3. **Michigan:** did the House take up SB 529-531?
4. **Arkansas:** 8th Cir. action on the preliminary injunction and the final judgment. Any stay?
5. **South Dakota:** is circulator residency enforced despite the 2023 ruling? The SOS guide says yes.
6. **California:** AB 421's operative date and whether paid-circulator registration covers initiatives or only referenda.
7. **Nevada, Illinois, Alaska residency, Idaho H0501, Nebraska LB1068:** not fully verified.
8. **Ohio:** status of SB 153 / HB 233 after Nov 2025.
