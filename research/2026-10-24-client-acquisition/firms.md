## Notes

- **Florida angle (important for a Florida-based pro):** HB 1205 (2025) requires paid circulators to be **Florida residents and U.S. citizens**, to register with the Division of Elections, and to turn in forms within 10 days. Organizations face fines of up to $50,000 per violation (Florida Senate bill summary; Orlando Weekly/Florida Phoenix, 2/23/2026). No citizen initiatives made the 2026 Florida ballot (ClickOrlando, 2/2/2026). Florida Decides Healthcare relaunched for 2028. Out-of-state firms will need Florida-resident leads for the 2028 cycle, which is a selling point.
- **Colorado:** since Dec 1, 2025, every paid circulator must be registered by the licensed entity with SOS training certified within the past year, and cannot be paid until approved (up to 10 business days) (CO SOS newRequirements page). A pro who is already trained and registered is valuable there.
- **Nebraska:** after the August 2026 fraud arrests, the Governor and Secretary of State are pushing to restore a ban on per-signature pay (WOWT, 8/7/2026). Expect hourly pay structures.
- Red flags in 2025-26 coverage: AMT (Missouri AG investigation), Victor's Canvassing (misleading pitches), Arno/Ohio (unpaid petitioners), Let the Voters Decide (sabotage lawsuit defendant).
