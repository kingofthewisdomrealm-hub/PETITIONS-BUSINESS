Top 20 petition prospects to call this week

Two kinds of buyers. Campaigns need signatures and pay for them. Firms win those contracts and need experienced leads to run crews. Ranked by deadline, money, and where your Florida residency gives you an edge.

Florida: no per-signature pay

It's a felony (s.104.186). Price Florida work hourly, daily, or as a flat project fee.

Florida: register first

HB 1205 requires paid circulators to be registered Florida residents and U.S. citizens. Fines run up to $50,000 per violation. Register before you pitch.

Other states have their own rules

Colorado, Arizona, Oregon, Utah, Montana and Missouri require registration or training. Massachusetts, Michigan and Nebraska may ban per-signature pay soon.
