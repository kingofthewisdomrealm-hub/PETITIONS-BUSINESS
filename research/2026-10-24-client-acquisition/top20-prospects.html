Compliance rules to know first

  1. No per-signature pay in Florida. It's a felony (s.104.186). Florida work gets priced hourly, daily, or as a flat project fee.
  2. Register before you work in Florida. HB 1205 requires paid circulators to be registered Florida residents and U.S. citizens. Fines run up to $50,000 per violation.
  3. Every state has its own rules. Colorado, Arizona, Oregon, Utah, Montana and Missouri require registration or training. Massachusetts, Michigan and Nebraska may ban per-signature pay soon.
